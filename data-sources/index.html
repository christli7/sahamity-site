Berita

  • Hasil pencarian Google News, hanya dari penerbit tepercaya. Indonesia: Kontan, Bisnis.com, CNBC Indonesia, IDX Channel, Investor.id, Katadata, ANTARA, Emiten News, Kompas, detikFinance, Tempo, IDN Financials, BeritaSatu, dan lainnya. Amerika Serikat: Reuters, Bloomberg, The Wall Street Journal, CNBC, MarketWatch, Barron’s, Financial Times, AP, Yahoo Finance, dan lainnya.
  • Feed langsung penerbit: CNBC Indonesia, ANTARA, IDX Channel, Kontan, dan detikFinance.
  • Laporan resmi: laporan terkini Form 8-K di SEC EDGAR.
  • Kantor berita siaran pers: PR Newswire, Business Wire, dan GlobeNewswire.

Hanya judul, kutipan singkat, dan tautan yang disimpan; artikel lengkap tetap berada di penerbitnya. Setiap berita menampilkan penerbit aslinya dan tautan ke sana.

Daftar perusahaan

Kode saham AS berasal dari daftar perusahaan publik SEC; kode saham BEI dari daftar referensi emiten tercatat, ditambah kode yang disebutkan secara eksplisit di judul berita.

Analisis AI

Penyaringan, klasifikasi, terjemahan, dan analisis dihasilkan oleh model Gemini dan Gemma dari Google berdasarkan berita yang dikumpulkan. Lihat Penafian investasi untuk batasannya.

Harga dan fundamental

Data fundamental AS berasal dari laporan XBRL di SEC. Riwayat harga, bila tersedia, berasal dari penyedia data akhir hari berlisensi dan tertunda, bukan waktu nyata. Rencana trading berbentuk persentase dan tidak memerlukan harga langsung.

Waktu dan penyimpanan

Feed Pro diperbarui setiap menit dan feed Gratis setiap 5 menit, tetapi berita baru hanya muncul jika berita yang memenuhi syarat benar-benar terbit. Berita, analisis, dan feed ditampilkan selama 3 hari (paket Gratis), 7 hari (paket Pro), dan dihapus otomatis 7 hari setelah terbit.

Where Sahamity’s information comes from, and its limits.

News

  • Google News search results, accepted only from established publishers. Indonesia: Kontan, Bisnis.com, CNBC Indonesia, IDX Channel, Investor.id, Katadata, ANTARA, Emiten News, Kompas, detikFinance, Tempo, IDN Financials, BeritaSatu and others. United States: Reuters, Bloomberg, The Wall Street Journal, CNBC, MarketWatch, Barron’s, Financial Times, AP, Yahoo Finance and others.
  • Direct publisher feeds: CNBC Indonesia, ANTARA, IDX Channel, Kontan and detikFinance.
  • Official filings: SEC EDGAR Form 8-K current reports.
  • Press-release wires: PR Newswire, Business Wire and GlobeNewswire.

Only headlines, short excerpts and links are stored; full articles stay with their publishers. Each item shows its original publisher and links to it.

Company lists

US tickers come from the SEC’s public company list; IDX tickers from a reference list of listed issuers, plus tickers stated explicitly in news headlines.

AI analysis

Screening, classification, translation and analysis are produced by Google’s Gemini and Gemma models from the collected news. See the Investment disclaimer for their limits.

Prices and fundamentals

US fundamentals come from SEC XBRL filings. Price history, when available, comes from a licensed end-of-day data provider and is delayed, not real-time. Trading plans are percentages and do not need live prices.

Timing and retention

Pro feeds update every minute and Free feeds every 5 minutes, but new items appear only when qualifying news is actually published. News, analyses and feeds are shown for 3 days (Free tier), 7 days (Pro tier), and deleted automatically 7 days after publication.
