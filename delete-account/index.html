Hapus akun Sahamity Anda

Terakhir diperbarui 2026-11-01 · sahamity00@gmail.com

Halaman ini menjelaskan cara menghapus akun Anda di aplikasi Sahamity, oleh Sahamity, dan apa yang terjadi pada data Anda.

Hapus akun sekarangDelete your account now

Jika akun ini ada, kami telah mengirim kode 6 angka ke email tersebut. Berlaku 10 menit.If this account exists, we have sent a 6-digit code to that email. It lasts 10 minutes.

Akun Anda telah dihapus.Your account has been deleted.

Hapus di aplikasi

  • Buka Sahamity dan masuk.
  • Buka Pengaturan → Hapus akun.
  • Konfirmasi. Akun Anda langsung dihapus dan tidak dapat dipulihkan.

Hapus di situs web

  • Buka sahamity.com/delete-account dan masukkan alamat email akun Anda.
  • Masukkan kode yang kami kirim lewat email untuk memastikan itu akun Anda.
  • Tekan tombol hapus. Akun Anda langsung dihapus dan tidak dapat dipulihkan.

Data yang dihapus

  • Alamat email, nama tampilan, dan hash kata sandi, atau identitas masuk Google Anda.
  • Preferensi bahasa dan tema Anda.
  • Sesi masuk dan status paket Anda.

Data yang disimpan

  • Catatan pembelian: tetap disimpan setelah penghapusan, terlepas dari akun Anda, sesuai kewajiban pajak dan akuntansi.
  • Catatan log keamanan, termasuk alamat IP yang digunakan: disimpan hingga 180 hari, lalu dihapus.

Langganan

Menghapus akun tidak membatalkan langganan di toko. Batalkan terlebih dahulu di Google Play → Pembayaran & langganan, atau di App Store → Langganan.

Delete your Sahamity account

Last updated 2026-11-01 · sahamity00@gmail.com

This page explains how to delete your account in the Sahamity app, by Sahamity, and what happens to your data.

Hapus akun sekarangDelete your account now

Jika akun ini ada, kami telah mengirim kode 6 angka ke email tersebut. Berlaku 10 menit.If this account exists, we have sent a 6-digit code to that email. It lasts 10 minutes.

Akun Anda telah dihapus.Your account has been deleted.

Delete it in the app

  • Open Sahamity and sign in.
  • Go to Settings → Delete account.
  • Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.

Delete it on the website

  • Go to sahamity.com/delete-account and enter the email address on your account.
  • Enter the code we email you to confirm it is your account.
  • Press the delete button. Your account is deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.

Data that is deleted

  • Your email address, display name and password hash, or your Google sign-in identity.
  • Your language and theme preferences.
  • Your sign-in sessions and your plan status.

Data that is kept

  • Purchase records: kept after deletion, detached from your account, as required for tax and accounting.
  • Security log entries, including the IP address used: kept for up to 180 days, then deleted.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or in App Store → Subscriptions.
